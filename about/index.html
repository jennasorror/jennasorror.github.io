A little about meA little about me

I'm Jenna, a Seattle-based product designer who came to UX through journalism. I studied Informatics and Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Washington.

One of my favorite parts of reporting was asking a follow-up question and hearing something I hadn’t expected. I still look forward to those moments in design. Getting to know the people using a product helps me see past my own assumptions, especially when something that seemed clear to me turns out to be confusing for someone else.

How I work

Clarity is an act of care.

I want the people using my designs to feel comfortable asking questions, and I want the same thing from a team. I enjoy working through an unfinished idea with someone who sees it differently.
