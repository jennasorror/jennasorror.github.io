A little about me

I start by listening.
Then I find the story.

I'm Jenna, a Seattle-based UX designer with a background in journalism and a degree in Informatics, focused on Human-Computer Interaction.

Journalism taught me to ask better questions, notice what people leave unsaid, and turn complex information into a clear story. I bring that same curiosity to design: understanding people's experiences before deciding what an interface should do.

From warehouse analytics to community volunteering, I care about making technology feel understandable and approachable. I work closely with researchers, product partners, and engineers to turn what we learn into accessible interactions that fit real constraints.

How I work

Clarity is an act of care.

I look for the moments where people hesitate: a chart without context, a task without confirmation, or a prompt that asks too much. Those moments guide my decisions. I value candid feedback, shared understanding, and collaboration that makes a design stronger than I could make it alone.
